FAQ

Quick answers to common questions.

What are the check-in / check-out times? ▾
Check-in is generally from 14:00, check-out by 12:00. If you need early or late times, please let us know in advance and we'll do our best to accommodate.
How do I make a payment? ▾
We accept cash, card, or bank transfer. For online bookings, a payment link will be sent to your email after confirmation.
Can you arrange airport pickup? ▾
Yes! Airport pickup and drop-off can be arranged for an additional fee. Please mention this in your booking request.
Is WiFi available? ▾
Yes — free high-speed WiFi is available in all rooms throughout the guesthouse.
What is the cancellation policy? ▾
In most cases, cancellations made more than 48 hours before check-in are free of charge. Later cancellations may incur a fee.
